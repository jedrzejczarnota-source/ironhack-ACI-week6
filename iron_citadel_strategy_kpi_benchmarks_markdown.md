## 3. Audience Structure & The "Apex" Economy

Our games are brutal. We accept massive early churn to filter for hyper-competitive spenders.

1. **Demographics:** **88% Male**, aged **25 to 50 years old**. Our highest-spending cohorts are business owners, finance professionals, and legacy wealth in the MENA region.
2. **The "Apex" Spender (Whale) Dependency:**
   * **Apex Players ($\ge$\$10,000 USD Lifetime Spend):** Represent just **0.5% of our active player base**, but drive an astounding **72% of total studio revenue**. We assign these players dedicated Account Managers via WhatsApp/Telegram to manually sell them $5,000 custom bundles and prevent churn.
   * **Officers/Dolphins ($500 – $9,999 Lifetime):** 4% of players, 22% of revenue. They buy the $19.99 weekly passes and $99.99 monthly progression packs to support the Apex players in guild wars.
   * **F2P / Cannon Fodder:** 95% of the player base spends $0. Their role in the ecosystem is to populate the map, gather resources, and serve as targets for the Apex players to dominate.

## 4. Internal KPI Benchmarks & Strategic Guardrails

### 4.1 Priority 1: Hardcore Retention & Monetisation Benchmarks
Unlike casual games, our D1 retention is terrible by design. The game is highly complex and punitive, weeding out players who won't spend.

| KPI Metric | Global Conflict (5y) | Empire of Ash (2y) | Studio Planning Target / Action Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D1 Retention** | 28.0% | 32.0% | **If D1 > 40%, the game is too easy.** Increase early upgrade timers. |
| **D7 Retention** | 10.0% | 12.5% | Target range: **9% – 14%** |
| **D30 Retention** | 4.2% | 5.1% | Low D30 is acceptable as long as ARPDAU remains above $3.00. |
| **D360 Retention** | 1.8% | 2.1% | Elder players must be locked into deep Guild sunk-cost loops. |
| **Blended ARPDAU** | $3.85 | $4.20 | Portfolio target: **$\ge$ $3.50 globally** |
| **US / MENA LTV360** | $110.00 | $145.00 | Massive LTVs justify our extreme User Acquisition costs. |

### 4.2 Priority 2: LiveOps — Attrition Warfare & Power Creep
* **Server vs. Server (SvS) Conflict:** Our main revenue driver is the bi-weekly "Kingdom Void" event, forcing entire servers to fight each other. Players spend thousands on healing speed-ups and troop revives in a 48-hour window to avoid losing their troops.
* **Intentional Power Creep:** Every 90 days, we release a new Hero or Commander in the Gacha pool that mathematically invalidates the previous meta. Apex players are forced to spend $1,500+ to max out the new character or lose their server dominance.
* **Web-Shop Exclusivity:** We push all heavy spenders off the App Store and onto our Xsolla-powered web shop to bypass the 30% Apple/Google tax, offering them 20% bonus gems in return.

### 4.3 Priority 3: User Acquisition (Aggressive & Misleading Creatives)
We spend **$120M USD/year on paid User Acquisition**. Because our LTV is so high, we can afford exorbitant Cost Per Install (CPI) rates to find the 1-in-1000 whale.
* **Target CPI:** We gladly pay **$45.00 to $85.00 CPI** on US iOS for value-optimized (VO) traffic targeting high-net-worth users.
* **Creative Strategy ("Fake Ads"):** We heavily utilize "bait-and-switch" creatives. We run ads showing zombie sniper puzzles, satisfying math-gate running games, or tower-defense minigames. While these mechanics exist as trivial 2-minute minigames in our app to comply with App Store policies, the actual game is a hardcore spreadsheet-driven MMO. This lowers our top-of-funnel CPI while we filter for spenders later.

## 5. How the Research Agent Should Use This Playbook

When analyzing competitors (like Lilith Games, FunPlus, or Scopely), the agent must:
* Evaluate if the competitor is utilizing new Gacha mechanics, VIP web-shops, or high-friction PvP events that Iron Citadel should copy.
* Ignore casual KPI metrics. If a competitor has high retention but low ARPDAU, they are fundamentally incompatible with our aggressive whale model.
* Recommend ruthless, high-ROI actions: increasing monetization pressure, expanding the web-shop, or adopting deceptive but effective UA creative trends.

The required recommendation format is:
**Observation (with source) → Implication for Iron Citadel's Whale Economy → High-Yield P2W Test → Success Metric (tied to ARPDAU or Apex Spend).**
