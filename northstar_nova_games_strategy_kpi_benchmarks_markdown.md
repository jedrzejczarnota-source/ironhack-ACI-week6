## 3. Audience Structure, Demographics & VIP Economy

1. **Demographics:** Across all three games, the player base skews **68% female / 32% male**, aged **34 to 58 years old** (median age: 44 in *Manor of Words*, 39 in *Bloomberry Bay*, 35 in *Velvet Bistro*). Over 72% of payers live in suburban or Tier-1/Tier-2 urban centers in the USA, UK, and DACH region.
2. **Play Patterns ("Cozy Achievers" & "Daily Ritualists"):** Players average **3.4 sessions per day** at **11 to 16 minutes per session**. Peak concurrency occurs during morning routines (7:30–9:00 AM) and evening sofa wind-down (8:00–10:30 PM local time).
3. **Spend Tier Breakdown & The $2,000+ VIP Segment:**
   * **VIP Players (Platinum Tier — $\ge$\$2,000 USD Lifetime IAP Spend):** Roughly **5% of our active paying player population** (and ~0.45% of total monthly active players) are VIPs. This 5% segment drives **54% of total IAP revenue**. VIPs receive dedicated 1-on-1 VIP account hosts, preview builds of new features, exclusive cosmetic frames, and invitation to the **Northstar Direct Webshop** (providing +15% bonus currency on bundles by avoiding 30% platform store commissions).
   * **Core Spenders / Dolphins ($100 – $1,999 Lifetime IAP):** **18% of paying players** (~28% of IAP bookings). Primary buyers of the $9.99 Season Pass, $14.99 Event Passes, and weekend multi-step offers.
   * **Light Spenders / Minnows ($1.99 – $99 Lifetime IAP):** **77% of paying players** (~18% of IAP bookings). Convert via the $2.99 Welcome Starter Pack (+250% value), $4.99 Piggy Bank breaks, and $1.99 "+5 Extra Moves / Save My Streak" offers.

### 4.2 Priority 2: A Repeatable LiveOps Learning Loop & Sales Design
We release small, measurable events regularly. Every LiveOps release requires a documented hypothesis, target player segment, and success metric.
* **Event Cadence:** 1× 28-day Season Pass ($9.99 standard / $19.99 Gold tier), 2× weekday solo progression events (48–72h duration: win-streak challenges, dig/board mini-games), and 1× weekend social/guild event (72–96h duration).
* **Promotional Sales Structure:**
  1. **Value-Expansion Sales (75% of promotions — Primary):** We keep established price points unchanged ($4.99, $9.99, $19.99, $49.99, $99.99) and grant **+150% to +300% more in-game currency, energy, or card packs**. This protects reference price integrity.
  2. **Price-Reduction Sales (25% of promotions — Secondary):** Direct strike-through price reductions on established bundles (e.g., a $19.99 bundle reduced to $11.99 for 24 hours). Used strictly for D14 non-payer conversion nudges, lapsed payer reactivation (30+ days inactive), and major holiday tentpoles.
* **Dynamic Offer Tiers:** Our LiveOps engine dynamically scales bundle prices based on prior spend history (from $1.99 entry offers up to $99.99 VIP vault bundles) so VIPs always have meaningful purchase depth.

Northstar spends **$58M USD/year on paid User Acquisition**. We strictly prohibit misleading "fake gameplay" ads (e.g., pin-pulling puzzles or extreme melodrama tropes not present in the game) that inflate D1 installs while hurting store ratings and D7+ retention. * **UA Budget Allocation:** AppLovin MAX & Moloco DSP (36% — interactive playables), Meta / Instagram (34% — video & reels targeting 35–55 females), Google App Campaigns (16%), Apple Search Ads (8% — high-intent keyword protection & competitor conquesting), and TikTok UGC Creators (6%).
* **Four Core Creative Campaign Designs:**
  1. **Authentic Core-Loop Playables (HTML5, 25–30s):** Interactive mini-levels mirroring actual gameplay (solving 4 crossword clues in *Manor of Words* or fulfilling 3 bistro merge orders in *Velvet Bistro*).
  2. **ASMR & Tactile Progression Videos (15s vertical):** Satisfying board organisation, cascading match combos, and cozy before/after room makeovers.
  3. **"Smart Daily Ritual" UGC Creators:** Real creators (aged 35–55) highlighting relaxing daily puzzle mastery and cozy design without fake failure rage-bait.
  4. **LiveOps Seasonal Reactivation Ads:** Targeted Meta and Apple Search Custom Product Pages (CPPs) showcasing new 28-day seasonal themes to re-engage lapsed D30+ payers.

### 4.4 Priority 4: Technical Quality & Store Ratings as a Growth Lever
* **Store Rating Floor:** Maintain **$\ge$ 4.65★ on Apple App Store** and **$\ge$ 4.55★ on Google Play Store**. Any drop below 4.50★ triggers a mandatory bug-fix and UX friction sprint.
* **Stability Guardrails:** **Crash-free sessions $\ge$ 99.65%**; Google Play **ANR (Application Not Responding) rate < 0.18%** (well below Google's 0.47% bad-behavior threshold); cold boot time **< 3.8 seconds**.

## 5. How the Research Agent Should Use This Playbook

Our team uses competitor research to answer four practical questions:
1. **What is the competitor doing now?**
2. **Which product, monetisation, and LiveOps choices appear to support its performance?**
3. **Which ideas fit Northstar's audience (34–58 casual players, 5% VIP segment) and capabilities?**
4. **What should we test next in *Manor of Words*, *Bloomberry Bay*, or *Velvet Bistro*, and how will we measure it?**

When comparing a competitor with Northstar Nova Games, the agent must:
* Identify the competitor's reported facts and cite the originating source (Tavily, GNews, or Apple iTunes API);
* Label App Store or public-data measurements as **observed storefront telemetry**;
* Use the benchmark tables in Section 4 as internal planning guidance only;
* Turn meaningful differences into **three concrete product, LiveOps, or marketing actions** mapped to specific Northstar games;
* Avoid claiming that a competitor feature caused a financial or KPI outcome unless a cited source supports that claim;
* State **"not available"** whenever a metric cannot be verified.

The required recommendation format is:
**Observation (with source) → Implication for Northstar (specify target game) → Low-Risk Test → Success Metric (tied to Section 4 KPIs).**
